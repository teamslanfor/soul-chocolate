Chocolate making workshop

A hands-on 2.5-hour experience in the Soul Chocolate kitchen. Go from cacao bean to finished bar, learning the history, the craft and the science along the way.

Book a public session Private & corporate
Part class, part tasting
What you’ll do

From cacao bean to your own bar

  • Learn the history, craft and science behind great chocolate
  • See the tools and machinery we use every day
  • Enjoy a guided tasting of single-origin bars from around the world
  • Make your own chocolate from scratch and choose your own toppings
  • Leave with a bag of what you made

Held at our chocolate kitchen, 20 Wagstaff Drive, Unit C, Toronto, ON M4L 3W9.

Good to know

Workshop questions

How long is the workshop?

About 2.5 hours, hands-on in the Soul Chocolate kitchen.

Do I take anything home?

Yes! You’ll leave with a bag of the chocolate you made.

Can you host private or corporate groups?

Absolutely: team building, birthdays, group visits and more. Send a group enquiry or email events@soulchocolate.com.

Can I give a workshop as a gift?

Yes, there’s a workshop gift card.
