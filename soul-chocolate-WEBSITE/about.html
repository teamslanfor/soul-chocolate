How it all began

Born in a tiny Toronto apartment

Soul Chocolate was born in 2015 out of our tiny apartment in Toronto. Our goal was simple: we wanted to make the best chocolate possible.

At first, we believed the best chocolate was simply the one that tasted the best: packed full of flavour and complexity. As we kept learning, we realized making the best chocolate went far beyond the flavour and the finished product.

It all goes back to the source

Fair for everyone involved

What good is the best flavour when those supplying your ingredients aren’t paid fairly? So we decided to source all of our cacao, and every single ingredient, in a transparent and meaningful way. Our ultimate goal is to buy cacao directly from the farmers themselves, and we work towards it every day.

Meanwhile we source through importers like Meridian Cacao and Uncommon Cacao, who use a transparent trade model with a detailed breakdown of every cost from farm to final warehouse.

Meet Kyle & Katie

Chocolate lovers who became chocolate makers

Kyle and Katie both grew up in the Niagara region of Ontario and hit the road in their early 20s to explore the world. It just so happens the places they love to travel most are some of the best areas for specialty cacao!

When they finally ran out of money and returned home, they packed their bags one more time and moved to Toronto, determined to learn all there was about chocolate making and build a brand of their own. And that’s just what they did.

Their dream? To move back to Niagara, find an old-timey barn to convert into “chocolate lab 2.0”, grow everything they add to their chocolate (lavender, mint, you name it), and share the experience with anyone who wants to learn.

Made with love, shared with love
