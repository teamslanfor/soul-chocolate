Privacy & cookies

This policy explains how Soul Chocolate uses information you share through this website. Purchases are made in our online shop, which has its own privacy policy.

What we collect

When you send a message or group enquiry, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, email, phone number and message.

How we use it

Only to reply to you. We never sell your information.

How messages are delivered

Forms are delivered to our email through the FormSubmit service.

Cookies

This site doesn’t use tracking or advertising cookies. Google Fonts and the embedded Google map may collect technical data under Google’s own policies. If you dismiss the cookie notice, your browser remembers that choice locally.

Contact

To ask about, correct or delete your information, email hello@soulchocolate.com.
