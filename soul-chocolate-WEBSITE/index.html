From bean to bar

Every step, by hand

Bean-to-bar means we make our chocolate from the cacao bean up. Here’s the journey every bar takes.

Harvest

Ripe cacao pods are picked by our farmer partners.

Ferment & dry

Beans are fermented and dried at origin to build flavour.

Roast

We roast each origin in small batches in Toronto.

Crack & winnow

Shells are separated to leave pure cacao nibs.

Grind & refine

Nibs are ground and refined with organic cane sugar.

Temper & wrap

Tempered, moulded and wrapped by hand.
